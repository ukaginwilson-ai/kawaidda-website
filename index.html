About Kawaidda

Located at Bubbale. Here for your flock.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Kawaidda’s poultry products and how to get in touch.

What poultry products and services do you offer?

We offer broiler breeder stock, day-old broiler chicks, day-old Kuroiler chicks, poultry feeds, hatchery services, and farmer support.

How can I check current availability and prices?

Availability and pricing can change. Call or WhatsApp our team, or send an inquiry through the contact form, and we’ll share the latest details.

How do I ask about or order chicks?

Send a request using the order form, or contact us by phone or WhatsApp. An order is not confirmed until our team contacts you to confirm availability, pricing, and next steps.

Where is Kawaidda Poultry located?

We are based in Bubbale, off Semuto Road, Uganda.

Do you provide hatchery services and poultry feeds?

Yes. Hatchery services and poultry feeds are part of our offering. Contact the team to discuss your needs and current availability.

What should I include in an inquiry?

Share your name, phone number, the product or service you’re interested in, and any questions you have. An email address is optional.

Still have a question? Contact our team.
